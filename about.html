About PracticalUtility

PracticalUtility publishes concise, specification-driven buying and maintenance guides for essential home systems (HVAC air filtration, mini-splits, dehumidifiers, and drinking water purification) alongside small-business operations software (field-service dispatch and automated invoicing).

Publisher & Editorial Standards

Founded by Puneet Sharma, an operations and Order-to-Cash leader with over 15 years of corporate process experience, every guide on PracticalUtility cites official manufacturer specifications, independent certification standards (such as IAPMO/NSF/ANSI 53), or verified regulatory notices.
