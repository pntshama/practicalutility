Reverse Osmosis vs. Gravity Carbon Filters: Which Low-Maintenance System Fits Your Kitchen?

Comparing countertop RO units and gravity-fed stainless systems on TDS reduction, filter lifespan, and annual cartridge cost.

Renters and homeowners increasingly search for no-plumbing water purification systems that remove dissolved solids, chlorine taste, and heavy metals without drilling into granite countertops.

Countertop Reverse Osmosis (RO) units—such as Waterdrop systems—use a pressurized membrane to strip total dissolved solids (TDS) down dramatically, while gravity carbon systems like Berkey rely on dual-stage carbon elements with zero electricity and long cartridge intervals.

Because replacement cartridges are purchased every 6 to 12 months, water filtration generates reliable repeat commissions. Waterdrop runs an official affiliate program covering systems and replacement filters, while Berkey Filters offers a 15% CPS commission with monthly payouts.
